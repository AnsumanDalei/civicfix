Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an issue?

Login to CivicFix and click Report Issue. Fill in the issue details, upload an image if available, and submit the report.

How do I track my issue?

Open your Profile to see reports you submitted, or browse All Issues to follow public progress.

Can I upload photos?

Yes. Uploading a clear image helps authorities verify the issue more quickly.

Is registration required?

Yes. Registration allows you to track reports, receive updates, and manage your submissions.

How is issue priority calculated?

Priority is determined using severity, location, number of upvotes, and issue category.

How long does resolution take?

Resolution depends on the responsible department. High-priority issues are processed first.
