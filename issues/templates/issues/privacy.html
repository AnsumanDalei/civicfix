Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

CivicFix values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and optional phone number.
  • Issue titles, descriptions, category, location, photos, support votes, and case history.
  • Messages submitted through the contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process civic issue reports.
  • To notify users about issue status.
  • To share a reported case and its updates with residents and assigned authority staff.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Cookies

CivicFix uses essential session and security cookies for sign-in and form protection. The application does not include an analytics tracker. Public issue pages show the report details, location, photo, case reference, and timeline; they do not show the reporter's username or email. Assigned authority staff can access case details to handle a report. Map tiles and optional photo categorization may be provided by third parties when those services are enabled.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services such as maps or email providers. These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal information.
  • Update your account information.
  • Request deletion of your account.
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns.

7. Contact Us

Use the contact form for privacy questions or account requests.
